03 About the model

What Qwen-Image 2.1 actually does.

Released September 20, 2026 by Alibaba's Qwen team, with day-0 support across Diffusers, ComfyUI, vLLM-Omni, SGLang, and LightX2V. Licensed under the Qwen Research License.

ARCHITECTURE

7B, 32 single-stream DiT layers

Mixed-granularity attention with prefix KV cache reuse for multi-reference editing.

TRANSPARENCY

Native RGBA output

The prompt decides whether the result carries an alpha channel. No separate matting pass.

EDITING

Up to 10 reference images

Plus circles, painted annotations, or separate masks for local edits.

Full checkpoint, quantization and tooling index: wildminder/awesome-qwen-image.

TROOPOD

TrooImage frames the official Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1 Space for internal use. Generation runs on Hugging Face's infrastructure, not on Troopod's servers.
